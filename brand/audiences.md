# Audiences — who we are talking to

> The third leg of the canon. `voice-guide.md` is how we sound, `DESIGN.md` is how we look,
> `rules.md` is what is allowed — this is **who is reading**. Structured, because free text
> cannot be measured: "developers", "devs" and "technical folks" are three unjoinable strings.
>
> Every brief names a segment id from this file. The writer reads it, the guardian checks fit
> against it, the designer shapes to it, and the board slices outcomes by it.
>
> **`success_rung`** is the field that matters most: it says which step of
> signup → activation → paid actually counts as a win for these people.
> Add a segment the second time a brief does not fit an existing one.

## solo-builder

- **who:** Solo founder, indie hacker, vibe coder — building a real product without hiring engineers.
- **job:** Ship something real this week and find out if anyone wants it.
- **language:** ship · MVP · validate · weekend project · launch
- **ignores:** enterprise compliance · team permissions · SLAs · procurement
- **success_rung:** activation — build and deploy one app and they self-serve to paid fast

## ops-builder

- **who:** Non-technical operator inside a company — ops, marketing, finance, HR — building internal dashboards, trackers and admin panels.
- **job:** Stop waiting on the engineering backlog for something small.
- **language:** spreadsheet · manual process · "IT said six months" · the team needs
- **ignores:** code quality · deploy architecture · startup-speed narratives
- **success_rung:** activation, then team seats — they expand sideways inside the company

## dev-accelerator

- **who:** Developers using it to skip boilerplate and prototype fast.
- **job:** Get the boring eighty percent — auth, database, CRUD, hosting — free, and keep control.
- **language:** scaffold · boilerplate · full-stack · own the code
- **ignores:** no-code framing (mildly allergic) · beginner tutorials · hand-holding
- **success_rung:** paid conversion — they evaluate fast and either pay or leave

## student-learner

- **who:** Students and educators reached through the academic partnerships.
- **job:** Learn to build with AI and finish a portfolio project.
- **language:** learn · course · project · portfolio · assignment
- **ignores:** pricing tiers · enterprise features · ROI
- **success_rung:** signup and activation — monetisation is downstream or institutional
